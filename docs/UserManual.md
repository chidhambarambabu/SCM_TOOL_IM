### 1. Introduction
This manual provides instructions for utilizing the AI-Based Food Waste Reduction and Donation Management System.

### 2. User Roles & Workflows
- **Food Donors (Restaurants, Supermarkets, Cafeterias):**
  1. Navigate to the **Donate Food** tab on the application.
  2. Fill in the food description, category, quantity in kg, expiration window, and storage conditions.
  3. Click **Submit Donation**. The donation immediately broadcasts to the Live NGO Board.
- **Charities & NGOs:**
  1. Open the **Live NGO Feed** tab.
  2. Browse available food donations with remaining shelf-life timers and storage details.
  3. Click **Claim Donation** to reserve food. A Pickup ID will be generated automatically.
- **Kitchen Managers (AI Waste Forecasting):**
  1. Navigate to the **AI Waste Predictor** tab.
  2. Select day of the week, expected customer footfall, past sales average, and weather/event conditions.
  3. Click **Run AI Forecast** to receive estimated surplus volume, recommended prep adjustment, and environmental savings.
- **Faculty / Evaluators (SCM Demonstration):**
  1. Navigate to the **SCM & GitHub Hub** tab.
  2. Review the live Configuration Items Register, Change Request audit trail, Git branching strategy, and FCA/PCA verification checklists.
